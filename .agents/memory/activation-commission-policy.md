---
name: Activation commission policy
description: First-activation commission scope and legacy-data treatment.
---

The seller's fixed amount or percentage is a one-time activation commission, independent of legacy service-category labels. Percentage uses the titular's monthly price, not a proposal total with dependents.

**Why:** The user requested an amount earned for each activated beneficiary configured in the seller registration; legacy service labels must not silently disable that explicit configuration.

**How to apply:** Preserve historical snapshots, do not issue retroactive payouts for existing beneficiaries or on reactivation, and require missing eligible-seller configuration to be resolved explicitly. Recurring service commissions are a separate concept and must not prevent a sale commission.
